**Software factories, built to operate in your organisation.**

We design, build and commission software factories that turn well-defined engineering work into verified, reviewable results. Not just coding agents: the infrastructure, orchestration, isolation, verification and operational controls that make them dependable at scale.

**Your infrastructure or cloud. Your policies. Your team in control.**

## What we build

### Phase 1 -- Autonomous execution

A complete, standalone capability for executing work your team has already defined and approved.

```text
Ready task → Isolated agent → Automated checks → Reviewable result
```

The factory provisions a clean environment, coordinates agent execution, runs your repository's checks and returns the change with evidence for human review. Failed checks trigger bounded retries or escalation, not silent promotion.

### Phase 2 -- Event-driven orchestration (optional)

Extend the factory upstream when you're ready to automate how work is discovered and prepared.

```text
Events → Context → Policy and decisions → Ready task → Phase 1
```

Issue updates, comments, messages, emails and other authorised signals can initiate a workflow. The system interprets the signal, gathers context, seeks approval where needed and feeds ready work into the existing execution factory.

**Phase 1 delivers value on its own. Phase 2 is an extension, not a prerequisite.**

## Engineered for real operations

- **Isolated execution** -- clean, reproducible environments with scoped access for each task.
- **Durable orchestration** -- scheduling, retries, state, limits and recovery across agents and workers.
- **Verification by design** -- tests, CI, acceptance criteria and review gates integrated into the workflow.
- **Human control** -- your engineers retain architecture, risk, approval and release decisions.
- **Infrastructure flexibility** -- existing hardware, refurbished servers, cloud or hybrid deployments, with explicit cost modelling.
- **Operational ownership** -- visibility, runbooks, training and handover so your team can operate what we build.

We bring hands-on infrastructure engineering, large-scale orchestration and practical delivery experience. Our focus is the whole working system -- not a demonstration that depends on constant expert supervision.

## How we work

**Assess → Design → Build → Commission → Enable**

We start with a suitable workflow, design around your environment and constraints, implement the integrated system, demonstrate success *and* failure recovery, and hand over the capability with evidence and operational knowledge.

Engagements can cover the complete factory or selected components, working alongside your team.
